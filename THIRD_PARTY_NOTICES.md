## NASA Open Science Data Repository — OSD-569

ASTROCARE's NASA Research Data explorer includes a transformed representation of the processed Complete Blood Count (CBC) subset from:

- Study: OSD-569, *Whole Blood Measurements from the SpaceX Inspiration4 Mission*
- Official study page: https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569/
- Study DOI recorded in the supplied study metadata: https://doi.org/10.26030/e0rx-sh52
- Source files in this project: `NASA_DATA/OSD-569/`
- Browser representation: `nasa-cbc-data.js`

The data are NASA OSDR research materials and are not original ASTROCARE-authored content. They are not covered by the Apache 2.0 licence for ASTROCARE code. Their use, attribution and redistribution remain subject to the OSDR terms, the study's file-level notices/licence metadata, and any rights held by the contributing investigators or data providers. Do not assume that every OSDR file is automatically public domain or CC0. Check the current study page and applicable file notices before republishing the raw archive or transformed data.

OSDR recommends citing the dataset using the citation provided in the individual study page's Citation panel. The DOI above is recorded in the ISA metadata supplied with the archive; verify the current citation on OSDR before final submission.

Suggested acknowledgement, subject to the study's citation guidance:

“Data are courtesy of the NASA Open Science Data Repository (https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo).”
